How to use Easel

Everything you need to design posts, slides, posters and worksheets. Read it top to bottom in ten minutes, or jump to what you need.

1Start a design

The home screen has four ways in:

Pick a size

Instagram post, story, YouTube thumbnail, presentation, A4/A5/A3, business card, worksheet, infographic, logo.

Custom size

Type width and height in px, mm, cm or inches. Print sizes in mm export at the right physical size in PDF.

Start from a template

14 ready-made designs (social, slides, print, school). Everything in them is editable.

Drop a file

Drop an image on the home screen to start a design at its exact size, or a .easel file to open a project.

Your designs are listed under Your designs. Use ⋯ on a card to rename, copy or delete one.

2The editor

  • Left bar: Design (templates), Elements, Text, Uploads, Photos, Draw, Background, Layers. Click an item again to hide its panel.
  • Toolbar above the page: changes to fit whatever you selected: font and colour for text, filters and crop for photos, fill and border for shapes. With nothing selected it shows the page background.
  • Top bar: File menu, Resize, undo/redo, save status, the design name (click to rename), version history, Share and Download.
  • Bottom: page thumbnails and zoom.

Moving around

  • Zoom with Ctrl + mouse wheel (or pinch on a touchpad), the zoom slider, or Ctrl 0 to fit the page.
  • Pan with the mouse wheel, or hold Space and drag.

Selecting and editing

  • Click to select, Shift-click or drag a box to select several.
  • Drag the corner handles to resize. Side handles stretch shapes and change the width of text boxes. The round handle below rotates (it snaps at 45°).
  • Pink and orange lines appear while dragging: things snap to the page centre, the edges and other elements. Hold Alt to switch snapping off.
  • Right-click anything for copy, paste, layer order, lock and more.

3Adding things

Click an item in a panel to add it to the middle of the view, or drag it to exactly where you want it.

Icons can be recoloured with the colour button. Stickers keep their own colours. Charts and QR codes stay editable: select one and use Edit chart / Edit QR code.

Shapes: the toolbar has Fill (including gradients) and Border (colour, weight, dashed/dotted, corner rounding). Lines: pick the style and the start/end heads (arrow, triangle, circle, bar).

4Text

  • Text panel → heading, subheading or body text, or one of the font pairings. Shortcut: T.
  • Double-click a text box (or press Enter) to type. Press Esc when done.
  • Toolbar: font (42 to choose from), size, colour, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, alignment, letter case (UPPERCASE, Title Case …) and spacing (letters and lines).
  • Select just a few words while typing and change their colour or size, and only those words change.
  • Dragging a corner scales the font size. Dragging a side changes the box width.
  • Effects: shadow, lift, hollow, outline, echo, neon, highlight, glitch. Each has intensity and colour settings.

5Photos

Frames

Add a frame from Elements, then drop a photo on it (from Photos, Uploads or your computer), or select the frame and click a photo. The photo fills the shape. Drop a different one to replace it.

Editing a photo

  • Crop or double-click the photo: drag the box or its corners, with presets for 1:1, 4:5, 16:9 and more.
  • Edit photo: 12 filters (B&W, vintage, warm …) plus sliders for brightness, contrast, saturation, vibrance, hue, blur and pixelate. Double-click a slider to reset it.
  • Shape: turn any photo into a circle, heart, star, arch … or round its corners.
  • Flip horizontally or vertically. ⋯ → Set as background fills the whole page with it.

Background remover

Select a photo → BG remover. It runs on your own device: the model (about 44 MB) downloads once, then it usually takes a few seconds. Nothing is uploaded. Undo with Ctrl Z if you don't like the result.

Licences: hover a photo in the Photos panel to see its author and licence. Wikimedia photos are mostly Creative Commons, which usually means you have to credit the author when you publish.

6Arrange & layers

  • The Layers panel (or the layers button in the toolbar) lists everything on the page. Drag rows to change what's in front, and use the eye to hide or the lock to lock.
  • It also has Arrange (forward, backward, to front, to back), Align to page, and exact size, position and rotation.
  • Several things selected: Align lines them up and spaces them evenly. Group (Ctrl G) keeps them together.
  • Lock (Ctrl L) stops something from being moved by accident, which is handy for backgrounds.
  • Transparency button: fade anything from 0 to 100%.
  • Copy style / Paste style (right-click): copy colours, fonts and effects from one element to others.

7Pages & resize

  • Add a page with + at the bottom. Hover a thumbnail to duplicate or delete it, and drag thumbnails to reorder. Switch pages with PgUp / PgDn.
  • Resize (top bar): choose a new format. Scale the content keeps everything proportional and centred.
  • Copy & resize leaves the original alone and makes a copy in the new size, e.g. an Instagram post into a story.
  • Clicking a template of a different size asks whether to fit it into the current design or open it as a new one.

8Animate & present

Animations

  • Select an element and click Animate in the toolbar. Pick an entrance: fade, rise, drop, slide, pop, zoom, spin, wipe or typewriter (text only).
  • Set Duration and Starts after. Giving elements increasing start times makes them appear one after another.
  • With nothing selected, Animate offers Animate the whole page: one style for every element, from top to bottom, with a live preview.
  • In the same panel set the page duration (automatic = animations plus a short pause) and the transition to the next page (fade or cut).
  • Preview page plays the current page right in the editor.

Present

Click Present in the top bar for a full-screen slideshow that starts at the current page.

  • Next page: →, Space, Enter or click. Previous: ←. First/last: Home / End. Exit: Esc.
  • Autoplay moves on by itself after each page's duration, which is handy for a screen at an event.
  • The controls fade away when you stop moving the mouse.

9Download

Click Download and choose:

The size slider multiplies the resolution (2× is good for social media). Several pages as PNG/JPG/SVG arrive as one ZIP. You can also export a single page or a range like 1-3, 5.

PDFs are made of images, so the text in them can't be selected. Everything is rendered in your browser, and nothing is uploaded for downloading.

10Saving & sync

Easel saves automatically a moment after every change. You can see the status next to undo/redo. Ctrl S saves right away.

Everyone

Designs and uploads are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data deletes them, so keep important work as a project file (File → Download project file).

Owner (signed in)

Designs also sync to the Easel cloud. They show up on every device you sign in on, and the status says Synced.

Signing in (owner only)

Only the owner account can sign in. Everyone else uses Easel without an account.

  1. Click Sign in at the top right of the home screen, or the person icon in the editor's top bar (also File → Owner sign-in).
  2. Enter your Owner e-mail and Password, then click Sign in. Let the browser save the password, and next time it's one click.
  3. Your designs on this device upload automatically. Designs from your other devices appear under Your designs with a green cloud icon and download when you open them.

You stay signed in on that device until you sign out. Sign in once on each device you use (laptop, phone …).

First time on a device, or forgot the password?

  1. In the sign-in window, click First time or forgot it? Set a password.
  2. Enter your e-mail, the one-time setup code (ask for a new one if you don't have it) and your new password twice (at least 10 characters).
  3. Click Set password & sign in. The code works once and expires after a few hours.
There is also Use a login e-mail instead, but Supabase's free mailer sends only a few e-mails per hour and often refuses. The password is the reliable way in.

While you're signed in

  • The status next to undo/redo shows Synced once a change has reached the cloud (a few seconds after you stop editing).
  • Saved on this device only means the cloud wasn't reachable. Your work is safe locally and syncs by itself when the connection is back.
  • Click the person icon (or Synced on the home screen) for Sync now and Sign out. Signing out keeps the designs on the device.
  • If two devices edit the same design, the most recently saved version wins, and older states are still in version history.

11Sharing

Click Share (owner, signed in) and create a link. Nobody needs an account to open it.

View-only link

People scroll through all pages but can't change anything.

Template link

People click Use this template and get their own editable copy. Yours stays untouched.

Links always show the latest saved state. The Share window lists every link with its view count, and deleting a link makes it stop working immediately.

Not signed in? Send the design as a project file or as a PDF/PNG instead.

12Version history

  • Open it with the clock icon in the top bar, or File → Version history.
  • Easel keeps a version automatically every 10 minutes while you edit. Save a named version any time, for example “sent to print”.
  • Restore brings an old version back and saves the current state as a version first, so you can always go back again.
  • Copy opens an old version as a separate design.
  • Owner: versions are in the cloud and available on every device. Everyone else: versions are stored in the browser.

13On your phone

  • The tools are in the bar at the bottom. A panel opens as a sheet. Close it with ×, by tapping the page, or by tapping the tool again.
  • Adding something closes the sheet automatically so you can see it.
  • Drag with one finger. For fine work the laptop is more comfortable. Easel is built for bigger screens first.
  • Sign in on the phone too, and designs sync both ways.

Put Easel on your home screen

Easel then opens full screen like an app, with its own icon.

  • iPhone (Safari): open Easel, tap the share button → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  • Android (Chrome, Brave): open Easel, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  • On a laptop, Chrome and Edge offer Install in the address bar, which gives Easel its own window.

15Problems & FAQ

My designs are gone.

Designs live in the browser you made them in. A different browser, a private window or cleared site data won't have them. The owner can sign in to get them from the cloud. Everyone else: keep a project file as backup.

I can't sign in.

Check the e-mail address. “E-mail or password is wrong” usually means a typo or a password that was never set on this account. Forgot the password? Get a new one-time setup code and use Set a password. “No valid setup code” means the code was already used or has expired, so ask for a new one.

“Saved on this device only” in the top bar.

The cloud couldn't be reached (offline or session expired). Your work is safe locally and syncs automatically when the connection is back. If it stays, sign in again.

Icons or photos don't load.

They come from free online services (Iconify, Wikimedia, Picsum). Check your connection and try again in a minute.

Background removal is slow or fails.

The first run downloads the model. Browsers with WebGPU (recent Chrome/Edge) are much faster. Very large photos take longer.

Fonts look different in the SVG.

SVGs embed the fonts, but some programs ignore embedded fonts. Install the font (all are free on Google Fonts) or use PDF/PNG.
