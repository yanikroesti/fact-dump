How this is calculated

Compounding. Yield accrues every day at APR / 365 on the compounded balance and sits as unclaimed rewards until a compounding event, which happens n times a year. Only compounded balance earns; pending rewards do not. That is what makes claim frequency matter.

APR vs APY. APR is the nominal rate before compounding, APY is what you actually end up with. APY = (1 + APR/n)^n − 1. Switch the toggle if the number you were quoted is already an APY — it is converted back with APR = n × ((1+APY)^(1/n) − 1).

Costs. The performance fee is taken from each claim before it compounds. Gas is a fixed cost per claim, converted to tokens at that day's price — so frequent claiming on a small position can cost more than it earns.

Price scenarios. Each scenario moves the price smoothly from today's value to the end value at a constant rate, so no scenario relies on lucky timing. Rewards are paid in the deposited asset and compound into it, which is how staking and most lending markets behave.

Break-even. Yield grows your token count by a factor G. You are back at your starting money when price has fallen to 1/G of where it started, so the drop you can absorb is 1 − 1/G. This figure ignores recurring buys, which each have their own entry price.

What this does not model: slashing, protocol insolvency, depegs, lock-up or unbonding periods, taxes, or rewards paid in a different token than the one you deposited.
