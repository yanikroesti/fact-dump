Pacing rule
▶
7:00–7:30/km all 6 laps. Not your 6:00/km fresh pace. Feels slow lap 1 — that's the point. Walk first 200 m, walk last 200 m. Every lap.
Speeding up lap 4 is the #1 mistake. You'll feel good. Don't. Lap 5 pays the bill with interest.
Food plan per lap
▶
Targets per rest window: ~300–400 kcal, 300–500 ml fluid, electrolytes. Front-load the eating in the first 30 min after finishing — your stomach closes down once you lie flat.
Friday 20:00 · pre-event dinner
  • Pasta with tomato sauce + chicken (~600 kcal)
  • Bread roll
  • 1 banana
  • 500 ml water
Friday 22:30 · light top-up
  • 2 slices bread + honey + peanut butter
  • 200 ml water (stop drinking after)
After lap 1 · 00:30 window
  • 500 ml water + electrolyte tablet
  • 1 banana + bread with honey
  • Espresso (caffeine strike #1)
  • Handful salted almonds
After lap 2 · 04:30 window · real breakfast
  • 500 ml water + electrolyte tablet
  • 2 eggs scrambled + 2 slices bread + butter
  • Bowl of oats with honey + banana
  • 1 coffee (LAST caffeine of the event)
After lap 3 · 08:30 window
  • 500 ml water + electrolyte tablet
  • 2 slices bread + peanut butter + honey
  • 1 banana
  • Greek yogurt with berries
  • No more coffee
After lap 4 · 12:30 window · real lunch
  • 500 ml water + electrolyte tablet
  • Pasta with tomato sauce + chicken (~700 kcal)
  • Side of bread, extra salt
  • 1 banana
After lap 5 · 16:30 window · easy on stomach
  • 500 ml water + electrolyte tablet
  • 1 energy gel or 2 tbsp honey
  • Rice cakes or bread with jam
  • 1 banana
  • Square of chocolate if you want
After lap 6 · celebration
  • Big water + electrolytes first
  • Whatever you want. Burger, pizza, Älplermagronen — earn it
  • Protein + carbs for recovery
Mid-lap fuel for laps 4, 5, 6: carry 1 gel or fruit bar in your pocket. Eat halfway through.
No new foods. Only things your stomach already knows. Not the day to try a new gel brand.

                If things go wrong
                ▶
                Blister: Compeed plaster mid-lap, keep going. Don't pop.

                Sharp localized knee/shin pain: Only reason to stop. Dull ache = normal.

                Nausea: Stop solids. Sip water + salt. Flat Coke in small sips.

                Want to quit (lap 2 or 5): Normal. Walk first km, reassess at km 2. 90% of urges pass in 10 min.

                Missed alarm: Start the lap late — shorter rest next window. Don't skip.

                Paracetamol OK. No ibuprofen (kidney stress during endurance).
