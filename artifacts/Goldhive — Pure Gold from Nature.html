A family that
speaks fluent bee.

From flower to jar

Slow craft, plainly told.

Four steps. No machinery between the hive and your spoon. This is how honey was made long before it became a commodity.

01 / 04
Free-roaming bees
Our colonies forage across 4,200 hectares of unsprayed wildflower, chestnut and lavender.
02 / 04
Single-origin hives
Each frame is traced to one hive, one site, one season — never blended across regions.
03 / 04
Cold-extracted, raw
Spun by hand at 28°C, gravity-filtered through linen. Enzymes and pollen left intact.
04 / 04
Ethically left behind
We harvest only the surplus. The colony eats first; we take what they can spare.
